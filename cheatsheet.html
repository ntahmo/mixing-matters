Always run the diagnostics

Mᵢⱼ ≥ 0   |   Σⱼ Mᵢⱼ = 1   |   QᵢMᵢⱼ = QⱼMⱼᵢ

For undirected partnerships, check **each pair** of flows. For directed movement or exposure, do not impose symmetry without justification.

Answers to frequent questions

Do unequal activity distributions across contexts make mixing invalid?

No. They only mean that you should retain the joint opportunity distribution, rather than multiplying marginal distributions by default.

Can I estimate reciprocity from the data?

The observations inform contact rates and partner preferences. Reciprocity is a structural consistency condition for modeled undirected partnerships; noisy empirical observations may require appropriate adjustment.

Should I always choose typology first?

No. A sequential factorization is a mathematical identity, and behavioral ordering requires separate justification.

Does passing numerical tests validate the model?

No. It establishes internal mathematical consistency—not empirical realism, identifiability, or intervention validity.

Teaching order: understand interaction units → compute joint opportunities → construct a simple matrix → test reciprocity → add preferences → examine sensitivity.
