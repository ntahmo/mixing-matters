Never skip the common checks

  1. Define the interaction unit and target population boundaries.
  2. Write the population–rate product in matching units.
  3. Check mathematical properties appropriate to the process, including reciprocity for undirected partnerships.
  4. Evaluate limiting cases and differences across correlated subgroup distributions.
  5. Compare scenarios if empirical evidence cannot identify the structure.
