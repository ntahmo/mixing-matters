Glossary

Plain-language definitions for the terms that often appear before anyone explains them.

Assortativity

Preference to interact with a similar group beyond what the baseline opportunities predict.

Balancing

Solving for a matrix with required symmetry and row totals without changing the intended interaction totals.

Conditional probability

A probability after restricting to a known condition, such as activity given a selected network.

Contact matrix

A table of contact rates or interaction shares between groups; always specify the unit.

Force of infection

Instantaneous hazard of infection for susceptible people under a specified transmission model.

Joint distribution

Distribution across combinations of characteristics, such as activity and meeting context.

Log-affinity θ

A parameter multiplying symmetric partnership weights by exp(θ) for a matching characteristic; not a preferred-mixing ε.

Mixing matrix

A numerical rule for how interactions or partner opportunities are allocated between groups.

Model boundary

Which people and interactions are represented, and which lie outside the simulated system.

Partnership end

One side of an undirected two-person partnership.

Preferred mixing

A specified blend of assortative selection and proportionate selection.

Proportionate mixing

Destination group selected according to its share of available interaction opportunities.

Reciprocity

For undirected cross-group interactions, both groups count the same total between-group interactions.

Row stochastic

Each matrix entry is nonnegative and every row sums to one.

Structural uncertainty

Uncertainty about the form of the model, not merely its numerical parameter values.

ε (epsilon)

A mixture weight between 0 and 1 in the standard preferred-mixing example.
