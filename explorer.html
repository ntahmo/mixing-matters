How to interpret your experiment

Proportionate mixing: every row is the joint destination opportunity distribution, even when activity proportions differ between contexts. Preferred mixing: an ε-weighted exact-group matching component is added without violating reciprocity. Two-axis mixing: separate activity and context affinities are balanced to the same group opportunity totals. Naive marginal product: included deliberately to demonstrate why an intuitive equation can be normalized but still scientifically and mathematically wrong.

The two-axis θ values are log-affinities, not preferred-mixing mixture weights. Equal numerical θ values do not mean equal observed within-activity and within-context probabilities. See Chapter 7 and reproducible code.

Interpretation boundary: these are expected partnership-end allocations. Integer realized partnership counts, age structure, partnership duration, and epidemic outcomes require additional modeling.
